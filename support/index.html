What does FerryOrNot do?

It compares taking the ferry against driving all the way around — timed door to door — using live Washington State Ferries schedules, delays, and drive-up space, plus current traffic. It tells you which is faster, when to leave, and whether you’ll make the next boat.

How much does it cost?

FerryOrNot starts with a 7-day free trial, then $1.49/month or $14.99/year (US; local pricing shown in the app). Payment is charged to your Apple Account; the subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → FerryOrNot, then change plan or cancel. You can also open the app and go to Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. Canceling stops future renewals; you keep access through the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open FerryOrNot, and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen (or in Settings → Subscription).

Why does it ask for my location?

Location is used to estimate your drive time to the ferry terminal and, optionally, to remind you as you approach a terminal. When you commit to a sailing, the app may ask for “Always” access so it can keep watching your drive while the app is closed. All of this runs on your phone; no location is sent to Chowen Labs. You can limit or turn it off in iOS Settings. See our Privacy Policy.

Is the ferry data official?

Schedules, vessel status, and drive-up space come from WSDOT / Washington State Ferries; traffic comes from Apple Maps.

FerryOrNot gives estimates, not guarantees. Schedules, delays, space counts, fares, and leave-by times come from third-party data that can be wrong or late, and conditions change. Always check WSDOT/WSF directly and allow a margin. Chowen Labs is not responsible for missed sailings, schedule changes, or fare differences.
