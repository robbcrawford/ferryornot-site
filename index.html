The question

Every trip across the Sound is a bet.

Bainbridge to Seattle is 33 minutes on the water or an hour and forty-nine around. Which one wins changes by the minute: I-5, a late boat, a cancelled sailing, and how many cars are already in the holding lanes.

WSF's own app shows the schedule and a space number — that's it. Map apps don't know when your boat is actually leaving, and none of them predict delays!

Only FerryOrNot looks at your whole journey, in real time, and tells you the quickest option.

How it decides

Door-to-door routing checks, every minute.

The ferry option is four legs: the drive to the dock, the wait in line, the crossing, and the drive on the far side. The drive-around is one long leg with live traffic. The app times both from where you are and recommends one, with the confidence earned by analyzing months of ferry data.

The edge

WSF's app doesn't tell the whole story.

WSF's drive-up number counts cars scanned at the booth against a schedule slot. When there are disruptions, that number quickly becomes unreliable. Our backend has watched every sailing on ten crossings, every minute, since summer 2026. Three things it found:

A · The cars their app doesn't count

Published 37% full. Actually over capacity.

When a boat fills or is cancelled, the cars already through the booth spill onto the next sailing, and their app's number for that sailing doesn't include them. FerryOrNot simulates the queue in physical order and says when WSF's number is misleading.

B · We kept counting

Their app stops at +5, but the boat is still loading.

WSF stops updating a sailing's count five minutes after its scheduled time, even when the boat is late and still taking cars. FerryOrNot's backend keeps counting and serves its own figure for exactly the time when WSF's app goes dark.

C · After a full boat

The next one sells out 54% of the time.

If the previous sailing sold out, the next one sells out about 54% of the time even when WSF's number still looks roomy. FerryOrNot warns; their app can't.

Delay forecasts only where they're proven.Each boat's last observed delay is carried forward, gated per route and proven by data. On a typical day this month the forecast cut the timetable's error from about 8 minutes to 3.5. Where it can't beat the schedule, you see the schedule.
Cancellations the schedule feed hides.WSF deletes cancelled sailings from its schedule feed rather than flagging them. FerryOrNot reads the adjustment feed and its own captured baseline, so a cancelled boat is struck through instead of gone. Most importantly, we account for the cars that were stranded by the cancelled sailing.

Decide to take the ferry?

Tap "I'm going," and the app checks your journey, every minute.

Choose your journey, pick your boat. From that moment, the journey is checked continuously until you board, in the app and on your Lock Screen. If our recommendation changes — we tell you! Privacy first — no data ever leaves your device.

Leave-by

A reminder that fires to the minute even if the app was killed. The card goes on your Lock Screen the moment you commit, with the boat, the deck count, and the leave-by time.

On the road

"You can't reach the dock in time" is computed from where you actually are, not where you started. When a warning stops being true, the card says so: "your 7:05 works again." If the boat fills before you'd board, you hear that too, and the next option that still works.

At the booth

As you approach the terminal, the Lock Screen card becomes your boarding pass: the real scannable QR, at tollbooth size, on the locked phone. No unlock, no tap.

Aboard

The journey watch ends itself when the boat departs. Nothing runs on the far side, and nothing runs between trips.

Questions

Before you decide.

Why pay when other ferry apps are free?

Because the comparison is the product. No free app answers "ferry or drive, from here, now", and the watch that follows a commitment isn't a lookup feature. The schedules, cameras and space counts are here too, so nothing is missing.

Is the drive-up space number a guarantee?

No. It's an estimate built from WSF's count and our queue model, and the app says when it's unsure. Never a guarantee.

Will it drain my battery?

The location session starts shortly before you need to leave and ends itself when the boat departs. Nothing runs between trips.

Does it work without signal at the dock?

The pass is cached, the timetable is on the phone, and the app says "Offline" rather than inventing a recommendation.

What about reservation routes?

Port Townsend–Coupeville and the San Juans are covered for schedules and status. The app never promises a drive-up spot on a reservation sailing.
